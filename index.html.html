Send an email about your trip

Two steps. Fix Caroline's email first, then write your own and send it.

Your task

Write an email to a friend or colleague about a real or imaginary trip you have planned, for work or for pleasure.

  • Say when and where you are going, and how you are travelling.
  • Say where you are staying.
  • Give your prediction for the weather.
  • Talk about your intentions and arrangements for the visit.
Write 100–120 words

Timetable

Intention & prediction

going to

What you plan to do, and what you think will happen.
